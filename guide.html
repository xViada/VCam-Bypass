How to use VCam Bypass

Make a virtual camera (OBS, ManyCam, DroidCam, iVCam...) look like a real webcam.

1

Detect your cameras

Click Detect cameras and Allow. This only reads device names.

2

Select the target webcam

Open Target webcam and pick your virtual camera. A target is required.

3

Choose a camera to emulate

Pick a real model under Emulated model, or hit New identity.

4

Microphone (optional)

Use Mic mask to match the mic to the webcam, or leave it on Auto.

5

Enable and save

Flip the switch on and click Save changes.

6

Verify it works

Open Test it from the popup to confirm the site sees the fake webcam.

💡
Reload after every change. Settings apply on page load, so reload the site whenever you change the popup config.
